About

Still early. Already moving.

How I got here

I started with programming fundamentals such as Java, Python, and JavaScript. Alongside coursework, I have explored technical training, IoT, MERN-stack development, and Python through internships and learning programs. These experiences continue to shape my interest in building useful applications.

I'm also drawn to IoT — the idea that code can control something physical is what got me interested in engineering in the first place, and it's a thread I want to keep pulling on alongside traditional full-stack web work.

How I learn

I lean on structured programs to build a base — a HackerRank certificate in Python, Cybernaut EdTech's Tech Trio Program (Python, C, Java, and a first look at Django and Flask), and a webinar on designing, developing and deploying with AI tools that gave me a real workflow in Figma and basic UI/UX principles. Then I try to immediately use whatever I learned on something small, which is usually where the real learning happens.

I also take part in learning opportunities beyond coursework, including workshops, webinars, and college activities.

What I bring

Consistency. I keep learning through coursework, training programs, internships, and personal projects.

Turning "basic" and "learning" into "comfortable." React, Node.js and MongoDB are current focus areas, and I'm building real projects instead of only tutorials to get there — see Projects.

What I'm honest about
